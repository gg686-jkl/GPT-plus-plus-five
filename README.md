# Plus Double Five

Planning a big Codex or ChatGPT Work session at **8:00 AM tomorrow**? Paste this into ChatGPT on your phone:

> At 4:00 AM tomorrow, say “Hi” to me once. Use GPT-5.6 Luna, or the cheapest available model.

That starts the five-hour usage window at about 4:00 AM, so the next window may begin at about 9:00 AM—one hour after you start working.

Change the times to fit your schedule. To place the next window one hour after you begin working, schedule the task four hours before your starting time.

This does not add or bypass usage. The scheduled task consumes some of the shared ChatGPT Work/Codex allowance, weekly limits may apply, and OpenAI may change how usage windows work. Check **Settings → Usage** before relying on the timing.
